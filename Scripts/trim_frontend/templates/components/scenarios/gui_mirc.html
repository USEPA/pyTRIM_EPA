Simulation Inputs
{% call data_preamble('mirc_inputs') %}

The following fields are required for all ingested products you intend to use in your simulation:

  • Exposure Profile
  • Chemical
  • Body Weight Percentile
  • Ingestion Rate Percentiles
  • Target Date/Time
  • The choice of setting or calculating fish concentrations
  • The numeric values on the right side of the form (starting with Average Vapor-phase Concentration in Air)

Recommended to receive site-specific values, if available:

  • The numeric values on the right side of the form (starting with Average Vapor-phase Concentration in Air) for all ingested products you intend to use in your simulation, except for:
    • Fraction of Airborne Chemical in Vapor Phase
    • Density of Air
    • Kd for Garden Produce
    • Kd for Livestock Feed
    • Fraction of Fish Consumption from Trophic Level 3.5
    • Fraction of Fish Consumption from Trophic Level 4
    • Fraction of Total Chemical Mass That Is Dissolved in Liquid Phase
  • Note that most of the numeric values will auto-populate based on selections for corresponding compartments and trophic levels, based on other data you enter on other pages and based on outputs from the transport model
